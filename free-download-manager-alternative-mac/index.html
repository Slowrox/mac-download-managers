Free Download Manager alternative for Mac

When FDM is enough, and when you want more.

The short answer

Free Download Manager is free and handles everyday files. If you want a manager made for Apple silicon that also downloads just the part of a long video, try Lynx Download Manager.

Free Download Manager

  • Free
  • Extensions for Chrome, Firefox and Edge
  • Resumes interrupted downloads
  • Apple silicon build: not stated on its site

Lynx Download Manager

  • Made for Apple silicon
  • Browser downloads caught and split into parts
  • Resume after sleep or restart
  • Just the part of a long video, as an editor-ready file (Pro)
  • 14-day Pro trial, then free Basic
