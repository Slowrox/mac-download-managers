The short answer

Pick a download manager that resumes after sleep or restart, splits files into parts, and catches the downloads you start in your browser. For plain files, free apps do the job. For IDM-style downloads plus just the part of a long video, use Lynx Download Manager.

Five things that matter

  1. Resume after sleep, restart or dropped Wi-Fi
  2. Multi-part downloads
  3. Browser catching through an extension
  4. Made for Apple silicon
  5. Free limits listed before you install
