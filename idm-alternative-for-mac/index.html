IDM alternative for Mac (2027)

Internet Download Manager has no Mac version. Here is what works instead.

The short answer

IDM runs only on Windows. On a Mac, use a download manager with browser catching, multi-part downloads and resume. Lynx Download Manager was built to work the IDM way on the Mac.

What people miss from IDM

  • Downloads leave the browser on their own
  • Each file split into parts
  • Broken downloads resume instead of restarting
  • A queue that keeps going

Mac choices

  • Free Download Manager and Motrix: free, good for plain files
  • JDownloader: powerful, heavier
  • Lynx Download Manager: browser extension, up to 32 parts on Pro, resume after sleep or restart
